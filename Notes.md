# Presentation
🌱 Project Presentation Instructions

⏱️ Format
10 minutes – Presentation
10 minutes – Questions, discussion & feedback
Recommended: 6–8 slides
All team members should contribute
Language: English

🎯 What should you present?

Your presentation should tell a clear story from challenge → idea → solution → impact.

1. Introduction - The Challenge – What problem are you addressing and why is it important?
2. Your Solution – What is your idea? What makes it innovative or interesting? 
3. How It Works – Explain the concept with diagrams, sketches, calculations, models, etc.
4. Sustainability Impact – What are the environmental, social and economic benefits? Are there trade-offs?
5. Implementation – How could the solution become reality? Who needs to be involved? Obstacles or Bottlenecks?
6. Take-Home Message – What is the one message we should remember from your project?

💡 Keep in mind

Make it visual, simple and engaging. Avoid text-heavy slides. Use pictures, diagrams, numbers, sketches or prototypes.

Use the international and interdisciplinary expertise of your team. Consider different countries, perspectives and conditions.

You do not need to have a perfect or fully developed solution. We want to see your thinking, creativity, teamwork and how you applied what you learned during the Summer School.

🌍 Most importantly

Be creative. Be critical. Think sustainably. And have fun presenting your idea!
